Confronted with five pages of unranked product cards and knowing that a wrong coating will instantly vaporize under pulsed laser power, the researcher shuts the tab and calls a custom optical coating fabrication shop with an eight-week lead time. ## The High-SKU Photonics Spec Matrix

Between plano-convex lenses, bandpass interference filters, polarizing beam splitter cubes, parabolic mirrors, and optomechanical cage mounts, catalogs regularly exceed 25,000 active SKUs.

In this precision physics domain, traditional storefront navigation collapses under three technical barriers:

1. **Spectral Range & Coating Angle Sensitivity:** An optic optimized for 0° normal incidence reflects completely different wavelength bands when tilted to a 45° turning angle. Conventional tag filters rarely capture multi-variable angle-of-incidence dependencies.
2. **Laser-Induced Damage Threshold (LIDT) Nuances:** Substrates behave drastically differently under continuous-wave (CW) power density (measured in W/cm²) versus pulsed fluence (measured in J/cm²).
