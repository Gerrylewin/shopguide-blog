## The dead-end problem in big catalogs

You have 5,000 or 50,000 SKUs. A shopper lands, types something vague, clicks a few filters, and leaves. Search returns too many results or none that match. Filters force them to know your taxonomy. The long-tail products stay invisible.

Most tools just add another widget. Path Explorer does something different. It sits inside the ShopGuide agent and turns those browsing signals into an active path.

## What Path Explorer actually does

It runs in the background of every session:

- Captures on-page actions (views, filters, dwell time) and chat messages
- Parses intent and scores confidence
- Classifies the shopper stage: discovery, comparison, validation, or ready to buy
- Picks the highest-confidence next action (clarify, shortlist, handle an objection, or nudge to cart)
- Updates live as new signals arrive
- Logs every step and outcome for your dashboard

No extra tagging beyond the standard Shopify setup. It uses the native Catalog API and storefront events you already have.

## Why this matters for merchants

High-SKU stores lose money to indecision. Path Explorer attacks that directly.

You get more completions from people who were already interested. You see the revenue it creates versus a control group. Support tickets drop because fewer people get stuck on "does this fit" or "where is X". Long-tail items surface because the agent matches intent instead of keywords.

It is not a new filter layer. It is the intelligence layer that makes the rest of your store work harder.

## A real session walkthrough

Shopper views three industrial parts, applies a couple of filters, then types "compatible with Series 900 blender?"

Path Explorer tags the intent as technical validation plus compatibility. It classifies the stage as Validation. It responds with fit data, price context, returns policy, and a short list of matching in-stock items. Everything logs.

If they buy, the revenue attributes as assisted. If they bounce, you have data for the next improvement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is this different from regular product recommendations?**  
Regular recs are often static or based on past views. Path Explorer is live, stage-aware, and action-oriented. It decides what to do next based on where the shopper actually is.

**Does it need custom code?**  
No. Standard Shopify API integration and the ShopGuide agent handle it.

**How do I see the numbers?**  
Dashboards show assisted conversion rate, revenue per guided session, AOV delta, and more. A/B tests are built in.

**What happens when confidence is low?**  
Safe fallbacks: clarifying questions or general guidance. High-impact actions stay behind confidence gates.

**Does it work for long-tail products?**  
Yes. Intent signals match specific needs to exact items and complements that complete the purchase.
