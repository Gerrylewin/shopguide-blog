## The High-SKU Discovery Bottleneck in Janitorial Supplies

In the commercial cleaning and janitorial supply industry, catalog complexity is a double-edged sword. Having 10,000 to 50,000 SKUs allows stores to be a true one-stop shop, but traditional faceted search breaks down when handling complex, multi-attribute specs.

- **Chemical Dilution Ratios:** Products vary from ready-to-use (RTU) trigger sprays to 1:256 ultra-concentrates that require specific proportioning dispensers.
- **Surface Material Compatibility:** Selecting quaternary ammonium vs. hydrogen peroxide formulas depends entirely on whether the surface is stainless steel, marble, vinyl composite tile (VCT), or anodized aluminum.
- **Regulatory & Safety Compliance:** Facility buyers must filter by OSHA Safety Data Sheets (SDS), EPA registration numbers, Green Seal certification, and VOC compliance by state.

When search bars rely purely on exact keyword matching, buyers typing "bleach-free floor sanitizer for vinyl" are routinely shown irrelevant results or dead-end pages. This friction leads directly to [**Inventory Debt**](/blog/inventory-debt-large-catalog-shopify-revenue), where high-margin specialty chemicals remain hidden deep within collection pages.
