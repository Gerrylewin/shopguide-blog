## The Technical Spec Wall in High-SKU Electrical E-Commerce

Traditional e-commerce platforms were architected for simple consumer goods with two or three variants (size, color, style). Electrical and wiring catalogs, however, operate on deeply layered spec dependencies:

1. **Multi-Variable Compatibility:** A single wire spool purchase requires matching lug sizes, conduit diameters, voltage drop calculations, and breaker terminal temperature ratings.
2. **Metafield Isolation:** Traditional Shopify search indices weigh product titles and tags heavily, but ignore rich technical metafields containing NEMA enclosure ratings or UL listing standards.
3. **High Support Overhead:** When shoppers cannot verify spec compatibility on-site, they submit support tickets or order incorrect parts, triggering expensive returns and inventory restocks.
