## The 10,000 SKU Spec Wall: Filter Traps vs. Agentic Commerce

Industrial automation catalogs suffer from extreme multidimensionality. A single electromechanical relay or solid-state switch possesses dozens of critical engineering attributes:

- **Coil & Operating Voltage:** 12V DC, 24V DC, 110V AC, 230V AC, coil tolerance percentage.
- **Contact Rating & Form:** SPDT, DPDT, 4PDT, silver alloy contacts, gold-clad low-level switching.
- **Terminal & Mounting Architecture:** 8-pin octal, 14-pin flat plug-in, PCB through-hole, DIN rail socket fitment.
- **Environmental & Duty Cycle Ratings:** IP67 sealed, NEMA enclosure class, switching frequency limits, ambient operating temperature range.

Traditional faceted sidebar filters break down when buyers need overlapping technical criteria. Forcing a buyer to navigate five nested dropdown menus across thousands of SKUs frequently yields zero-result pages.

Instead of subjecting engineers to manual catalog navigation, **agentic commerce** enables customers to state their technical requirements directly: _"I need a 24V DC plug-in relay, 10A switching, DPDT contacts, with a matching 8-pin DIN rail socket."_

## Driving Higher Order Values Through Technical Compatibility

In industrial supply, Average Order Value (AOV) depends on complete bill-of-materials (BOM) fulfillment. A customer purchasing a control relay also needs the corresponding DIN rail socket, retaining clips, marking tags, and surge suppression modules.

Legacy recommendation engines rely on historical co-purchase algorithms. These often fail for technical catalogs because they recommend unrelated components bought by different contractors. An on-page agentic shopping assistant analyzes real-time technical context to recommend matching accessories that ensure circuit compatibility.

When an AI agent verifies terminal pitch, coil voltage, and mounting compatibility in real time, technical buyers purchase complete component bundles with full confidence.
