## The High-SKU Discovery Bottleneck in Outdoor Power Equipment

In the outdoor power equipment and small engine parts industry, catalog depth is a competitive advantage. Offering 10,000 to 50,000 SKUs positions a brand as a complete replacement hub, but legacy keyword search engines struggle with complex technical fitment data.

- **Engine Code Variations:** Small engine parts depend on precise model, type, and code numbers stamped directly onto engine blocks.
- **Deck & Spindle Dimensions:** Mower deck components require matching cutting width, blade arbor shapes (5-point star, 6-point star, or round), and spindle heights.
- **Cross-Reference Complexity:** Replacement parts span OEM part numbers, aftermarket equivalents, and superseded part strings from discontinued models.

When search bars rely on literal string matching, typing "belt for 24HP Briggs engine on 52 inch deck" often yields irrelevant accessories or empty pages. This friction results in severe catalog bloat where high-margin replacement spindles, air filters, and tune-up kits sit undiscovered deep in collection pages.
