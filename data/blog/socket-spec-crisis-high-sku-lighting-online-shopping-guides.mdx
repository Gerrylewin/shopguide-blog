## The High-Liability Spec Wall in Lighting Commerce

Selling lighting and electrical fixtures on Shopify Plus is incredibly lucrative, but it comes with a massive technical liability. Unlike buying a standard t-shirt, purchasing an electrical component requires exact physical and electrical compatibility.

A commercial lighting distributor with over 10,000 SKUs might have dozens of products that look identical but have completely different internal specifications:

- **Socket Bases:** E26 (medium), E12 (candelabra), GU10 (twist-and-lock), or bi-pin configurations.
- **Color Temperature (CRI & Kelvin):** 2700K (warm white) vs. 4000K (cool white) vs. 5000K (daylight), along with Color Rendering Index (CRI) requirements.
- **Electrical Standards:** 120V residential line voltage vs. 277V commercial voltage, along with low-voltage (12V/24V) drivers.
- **Dimming Protocols:** ELV (electronic low voltage), MLV (magnetic low voltage), 0-10V, or TRIAC.

When a customer gets overwhelmed by these options, they suffer from choice paralysis. They either abandon the cart entirely or order multiple variations with the intention of returning what doesn't fit. For high-volume merchants, these post-purchase returns destroy shipping margins and swamp support teams.

By deploying active **online shopping guides**, merchants can turn this technical specification wall into a seamless conversational flow.
