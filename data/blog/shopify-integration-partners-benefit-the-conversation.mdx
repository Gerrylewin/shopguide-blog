Merchants keep saying the same thing.

Not more apps. The ones they already pay for should work together.

On r/shopifyDev in February 2026, the top reply to "what are merchants actually struggling with" was short. Messy integrations. Too many apps doing half-jobs. "Merchants don't want more tools they want ones that actually work together."

A store with 18 apps showed up on r/shopify_growth the same month. Individually each one made sense. Together they slowed the store and confused the backend. Nobody on the team could say what half of them even did anymore.

Loyalty gets the same treatment. A points program that existed but was invisible. Customers could not see a balance without logging in. "A loyalty program nobody interacts with is just a cost centre."

That is the job of Shopify integration partners. Not a fifteenth widget. The paid context shows up where the shopper already is.

## The feature: partners ride the on-page conversation

ShopGuide is one theme block. You drop the ShopGuide Chat section into the theme editor. It opens on the page the shopper is on. It reads the live Shopify Catalog API during the chat so stock, variants, and price match what is actually available.

Shopify integration partners attach to that same conversation. They do not get a second chat window. They do not get a new script farm.

The list that exists today is on the [integrations page](https://blog.yourshopguide.com/integrations). Klaviyo. Judge.me. Rivyo. Smile.io. Alia. Slack. Plus the Catalog API, which is native.

| Partner | What you already bought it for | What ShopGuide does with it | Merchant payoff |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Shopify Catalog API** | Products, stock, price | Live lookup during the reply | Answers match the warehouse, not last week's snapshot |
| **Klaviyo** | Email, SMS, popups | Captures the email, tags the profile, fires named events | The conversation does not die when they leave |
| **Judge.me** | Verified reviews | Pulls a matching review and photo proof into the answer | Social proof at the decision, not below the fold |
| **Rivyo** | Reviews and UGC | Surfaces the same proof if Rivyo is your review app | Same trust job, different app |
| **Smile.io** | Points and VIP tiers | Reads live balance and tier | Points become a sentence instead of a forgotten badge |
| **Alia** | Personalized incentives | Keeps the offer in context after the popup closes | A closed modal is not a dead offer |
| **Slack** | Team comms | Posts customer, cart, and a transcript link | Human picks up with context instead of "describe your issue" |

That is the benefit. The review that answers "does this run large" shows up in chat. Points become "you have 500 points, that is $5 off today." A dismissed popup is not gone. A human gets the thread instead of starting from zero.

On a small catalog this is convenient. On a large one it is how the same question stops becoming a ticket.

## How ShopGuide is integrated into Shopify

Three connections. The partners only work because these three exist.

**1. Theme block.**
The agent lives on the storefront. Launch messages, visibility, and styling live in the app admin. One surface. Not a new embed for every partner.

**2. Shopify Admin.**
The dashboard sits in the admin sidebar. Home, analytics, Test your Guide, chat history, Integrations, Settings. Revenue attributed to chat sessions shows up there. No second login.

**3. Catalog API at talk time.**
The agent looks up products while the shopper is talking. Stock, variants, metafields. Partner context attaches to that same turn. A sold-out SKU does not get recommended as available.

Open **ShopGuide → Settings → Integrations**. The app scans the published theme for embeds it recognizes. Those apps land at the top under "Looks like you use these," with an On your store badge. That saves hunting through logos. It does not connect anything by itself. You still paste the key or token.

Most pairings activate when both apps are installed and you paste the credential. Slack needs an incoming webhook URL. Alia is on when your polls are active.

You do not have to connect every partner. A store on Judge.me and Klaviyo does not need Rivyo or Alia for the agent to be useful.

## Honest limits

ShopGuide does not replace Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, or Slack. If the partner app is missing or misconfigured, there is nothing to read. Installing ShopGuide does not remove the scripts those apps already inject.

Detection is a heuristic. It sees app embeds on the published theme. An app installed but not on the theme will not badge itself. Connect it by hand.

Reviews in chat are only as good as the reviews you collected. An empty review catalog means empty social proof.

Smile.io only helps once the shopper is identified, and only for what the token can read. A first-time anonymous visitor has no points to surface.

Klaviyo still owns the flow. ShopGuide hands it the moment. Follow-up only works if the shopper gives an email.

Slack is a one-way webhook. Your team resolves in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin, not by replying in Slack.

The Catalog API keeps stock honest. It does not write your descriptions. Dirty product data still produces thin answers.

Shopify integration partners are context in the path. They are not a new operating system for the store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are Shopify integration partners in ShopGuide?**
The apps ShopGuide can read from or write to during a guided session: Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, and Slack, plus the native Shopify Catalog API. The public list lives on the [integrations page](https://blog.yourshopguide.com/integrations).

**How is ShopGuide integrated into Shopify?**
Three places. A theme block on the storefront. A native app in the Shopify admin for analytics and settings. Live reads from the Shopify Catalog API during chat. Partners attach to that same conversation.

**Do I have to replace my current apps?**
No. Keep the apps that already work. ShopGuide reads them. It does not ask you to rip Klaviyo or Judge.me out.

**How do I turn a partner on?**
Open Settings → Integrations. Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, and Smile.io each need an API key or token pasted on their card. Slack needs an incoming webhook URL. Alia is on when polls are active.

**Does ShopGuide find my apps automatically?**
It badges apps it recognizes on your published theme. You still paste the credentials.

**Will this add more scripts and slow the storefront?**
ShopGuide is one theme block. It does not install a second copy of Klaviyo or Judge.me. Partner apps keep their own scripts. The integration is the agent reading those systems.

**What if I only use some of the partners?**
Use the ones you have. Partial stacks still work.

**Does Slack let my team reply inside Slack?**
No. Slack gets an alert with customer, cart, and a transcript link. Resolution stays in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin so the history lives in one place.
