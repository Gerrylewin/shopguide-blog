Merchants are tired of half-jobs.

On Reddit the pattern is the same. Someone installs Klaviyo, a review app, a loyalty program, and a support tool. Each one works. Together they do not talk. The shopper closes a popup, the points sit unused, the review lives under the fold, and the team gets a ticket with no context.

That is the real complaint. Not that there are too many apps. That the apps they already bought never show up at the same time.

ShopGuide's integrations page lists the partners that attach to the on-page agent today. Six of them. No coming-soon row.

## The feature: one conversation that already knows your stack

ShopGuide is one theme block. You drop the ShopGuide Chat section into the theme editor. Launch message, visibility, and styling live in the Shopify admin. One surface.

While the shopper talks, the agent reads the live Shopify Catalog API. Stock. Variants. Price. Partner context attaches to that same reply.

**Judge.me and Rivyo.** Verified reviews and photo proof land inside the answer instead of under the fold. You paste a public API token. No token, no review text.

**Klaviyo.** The conversation does not die when the visitor leaves. Newsletter capture on the storefront. Profile tags from what they asked. Named events the docs list: ShopGuide Chat Engaged, ShopGuide Order Attributed, ShopGuide NPS Submitted. Klaviyo still owns the flow.

**Smile.io.** Live points balance. VIP tier. What Gold actually gets them. A shopper can ask how many points they have and get a number. You generate an API token in Smile and paste it on the card.

**Alia.** Personalized incentives stay in context after the popup closes. Alia stays the personalization engine. ShopGuide reads what you already decided to show that visitor.

**Slack.** Stuck thread. High cart. "I want a human." Slack gets the alert with customer, cart, and a transcript link. Your team does not reply inside Slack. Resolution stays in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin so the history lives in one place.

That is the merchant payoff. The review that closes the sale shows up in the chat. Dormant points become a sentence. A closed popup is not a dead offer. A human picks up with the transcript instead of "please describe your issue."

## How ShopGuide is integrated

Three connections. That is the product.

**Theme block.** One storefront surface. Not a new script farm for every partner.

**Shopify Admin.** The dashboard sits in the admin sidebar. Home. Analytics. Test your Guide. Chat history. Integrations. Settings. Revenue attributed to chat sessions shows up there. No second login.

**Catalog API at talk time.** The agent looks up products during the conversation. Partner context attaches to that same reply. Sold-out SKUs should not get recommended as available.

Open ShopGuide → Settings → Integrations and the app scans your published theme for embeds it recognizes. Detected apps land at the top under "Looks like you use these" with an On your store badge. The badge is a map. It does not invent credentials. You still paste a key or token for Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, and Smile.io. Slack still wants an incoming webhook URL. Alia is on by default when your Alia polls are active.

You do not have to connect every logo. A store on Judge.me and Klaviyo does not need Rivyo for the agent to be useful.

## What this will not do

ShopGuide does not replace Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, or Slack. If the partner app is missing or misconfigured, there is nothing to read.

Detection is a heuristic. It sees app embeds on the published theme. An app installed but not on the theme will not badge itself. Connect it by hand.

Reviews in chat are only as good as the reviews you collected. An empty review catalog means empty social proof.

Smile.io only helps once the shopper is identified. A first-time anonymous visitor has no points to surface.

Klaviyo still owns the email. Follow-up only works if the shopper gives an address.

Slack is a one-way webhook. ShopGuide posts context into a channel. Your team resolves in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin.

The Catalog API keeps stock honest. It does not write your descriptions. Thin product data still produces thin answers.

Shopify integration partners here are context in the path. They are not a new operating system for the store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What Shopify integration partners does ShopGuide support?**
Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, and Slack, plus the native Shopify Catalog API. The public list is on the [integrations page](https://blog.yourshopguide.com/integrations).

**How is ShopGuide integrated into Shopify?**
Three places. A theme block on the storefront. A native app in the Shopify admin for analytics and settings. Live reads from the Shopify Catalog API during chat. Partners attach to that same conversation.

**Do I have to replace my current apps?**
No. Keep the apps that already work. ShopGuide reads them. It does not ask you to rip Klaviyo or Judge.me out.

**How do I turn a partner on?**
Open Settings → Integrations. Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, and Smile.io each need an API key or token pasted on their card. Slack needs an incoming webhook URL. Alia is on by default.

**Does ShopGuide find my apps automatically?**
It badges apps it recognizes on your published theme so you know which cards to fill in. You still paste the credentials.

**Will this add more scripts and slow the storefront?**
ShopGuide is one theme block. It does not install a second copy of Klaviyo or Judge.me. Partner apps keep their own scripts. The integration is the agent reading those systems.

**What if I only use some of the partners?**
Use the ones you have. Partial stacks still work.
