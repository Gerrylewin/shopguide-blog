## Why Keyword Search Fails Technical Fluid Transfer Catalogs

Industrial hose assemblies, hydraulic lines, and fluid control valves operate under extreme physical constraints. When B2B engineers and purchasing agents browse technical catalogs, their purchasing criteria depend on interdependent variables:

1. **Chemical Compatibility & Lining:** A nitrile hose handles petroleum derivatives but disintegrates when exposed to nitric acid. Fluoropolymer (PTFE) or UHMWPE linings are mandatory for aggressive solvents.
2. **Pressure Ratings & Safety Factors:** High-pressure hydraulic hoses require 4-to-1 safety burst margins, meaning a 3,000 PSI operating line demands a hose tested to 12,000 PSI burst thresholds.
3. **End Fitting Interoperability:** A hose body with a 2-inch ID must match specific stem dimensions, ferrule crimp specs, and thread pitches (such as NPT, JIC 37° flare, or BSPP parallel threads).

When these engineering constraints reside across nested variant options, custom metafields, and technical PDFs, legacy faceted filter apps collapse.
