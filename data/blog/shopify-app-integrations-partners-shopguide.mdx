Merchants are tired of paying for apps that do not talk to each other.

That is the quiet complaint under every "too many apps" thread on r/shopify. Reviews live in one tab. Points live in another. Email lives in a third. The shopper is still staring at a product page trying to decide.

ShopGuide does not add a seventh app to that pile. It sits on the page and reads the ones you already run.

## The feature: partners in the conversation

The public list is six: Rivyo, Judge.me, Klaviyo, Smile.io, Alia, Slack. You can see the cards on the [integrations page](https://blog.yourshopguide.com/integrations). Each one leads with the concrete thing it does for the merchant.

Automatic detection finds the embeds on your published theme and badges them. You still paste a token for most of them. Detection just stops you from hunting through a wall of logos.

That is the whole product around the partners. One conversation. Live catalog data. Partner facts when they exist.

## How ShopGuide is wired into Shopify

Three places. Not thirty.

The agent is a theme block. You drop the ShopGuide Chat section in the theme editor. Launch messages and styling live in the app admin. It is one surface on the storefront.

The dashboard lives in the Shopify admin sidebar. Analytics, chat history, integrations, settings. Same login as the rest of the store.

During the conversation the agent reads the Shopify Catalog API. Stock, variants, price. Partner context attaches to the same turn. A review quote. A points balance. A list capture. A Slack ping when the agent is stuck.

The shopper does not open a new tab to get any of it.

## What each partner actually does

Keep the ones you run. Leave the rest alone.

**Judge.me and Rivyo.** Verified reviews and photo proof inside the answer. A shopper asks which moisturizer works for sensitive skin. The agent can quote a real review instead of inventing confidence. Paste the public API token. No token, no review text.

**Klaviyo.** The conversation does not die when the visitor leaves. Newsletter capture. Profile tags from what they asked. Named events the docs actually list. Klaviyo still owns the flow. ShopGuide hands it the moment.

**Smile.io.** Points balance, VIP tier, what Gold actually gets them. The shopper can ask how many points they have and get a number. Generate the token in Smile.io and paste it. No token, the agent cannot see the balance.

**Alia.** Openings and picks that match the incentive you already decided to show that visitor. Alia stays the personalization engine. ShopGuide reads the context so the first line is not generic.

**Slack.** Stuck thread, high cart, "I want a human." Slack gets the alert with customer, cart, and a transcript link. Your team does not reply inside Slack. Resolution stays in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin so the history lives in one place.

That is the list. No vapor. No "coming soon."

## What merchants say they want to read

The useful sentence under the app-bloat threads is not "install more." It is "make the ones I already pay for talk."

One store ran 18 apps and cleaned the stack instead of adding another. Another operator capped at 10 to 12 and purged quarterly. The common complaint is that the tools do not share context when the shopper is deciding.

ShopGuide is built for that sentence. It does not uninstall your stack. It does not replace Klaviyo or Judge.me. It finds them on the theme you already published, then lets those facts ride the same conversation as the catalog.

Limits, said plainly. Detection only sees app embeds on the published theme. An app installed but not on the theme will not badge itself. Connect it by hand. Dirty product data still produces thin answers. The Catalog API keeps stock honest. It does not write your descriptions. Slack is one-way. Most partners need a key or token. A partial stack still works.

That is the deal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are ShopGuide integration partners?**
The apps ShopGuide can read from or write to during a guided session: Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, and Slack, plus the native Shopify Catalog API. The public list is on the [integrations page](https://blog.yourshopguide.com/integrations).

**How is ShopGuide integrated into Shopify?**
Three places. A theme block on the storefront. A native app in the Shopify admin for analytics and settings. Direct reads from the Shopify Catalog API during chat. Partner apps attach to that same conversation.

**Do I have to replace my current apps?**
No. Keep the apps that already work. ShopGuide reads them. It does not ask you to rip Klaviyo or Judge.me out.

**Will this add more scripts and slow the storefront?**
ShopGuide is one theme block. Partners are not copied onto the page as extra pixels. The agent reads those systems during the conversation.

**How do I turn a partner on?**
Detection badges what it finds on the published theme. Klaviyo, Smile.io, Judge.me, and Rivyo still need an API key or token pasted on their card. Slack needs an incoming webhook URL. Alia is on by default.

**What if I only use some of the partners?**
Use the ones you run. A partial stack still works. You do not need every logo on the page.

**Does Slack let my team reply inside Slack?**
No. Slack gets an alert with customer, cart, and a transcript link. Resolution stays in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin so the history lives in one place.
