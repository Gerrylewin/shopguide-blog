## Why Packaging Catalogs Cause "Specification Fatigue"

Ordering shipping materials is an engineering decision disguised as purchasing. Choosing the wrong box burst strength or tape adhesive causes crushed pallets, damaged goods, and costly carrier claims.

When packaging e-commerce stores scale past 10,000 SKUs, three critical friction points drive buyers away:

1. **The ECT vs. Mullen Test Confusion:** Procurement managers search by performance metric (e.g., "32 ECT vs 200# Mullen burst strength"), but titles often list only dimensions. Traditional search engines fail to bridge dimensional metadata with strength equivalents.
2. **Dimensional Tolerance Overload:** A single box style might exist in 180 size variations. Standard collection filters force users to pick exact inches, missing nearby compatible dimensions (such as 18x12x12 vs 18x12x10) that fit the same master carton.
3. **Environmental & Material Compatibility:** Thermal shipping supplies, biodegradable dunnage, and ESD-safe anti-static poly bags require explicit compliance verification before purchasing.

### Knowledge Takeaways

- **Specification Matching:** An active shop guide parses detailed product descriptions and metafields to identify flute types (A, B, C, E, micro-flute) and strength ratings automatically.
