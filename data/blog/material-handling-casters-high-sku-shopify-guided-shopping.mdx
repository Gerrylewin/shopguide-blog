## The Technical Spec Bottleneck in Material Handling Catalogs

In material handling, warehousing, and industrial hardware, catalog breadth is essential for serving B2B clients. However, when store inventory scales past 10,000 SKUs, traditional faceted filtering breaks down under the weight of multi-layered technical specs:

- **Mounting Dimensions & Stem Specs:** Caster selection hinges on exact measurements—grip ring stems, threaded stems, or top plate bolt hole patterns measured down to fractions of an inch.
- **Load Capacity & Weight Distribution:** Buyers must calculate static vs. dynamic weight capacity per caster based on total load and wheel configuration.
- **Wheel Composition & Floor Protection:** Choosing non-marking rubber, moldon polyurethane, forged steel, or phenolic resin depends entirely on floor material, chemical exposure, and operating temperature.

When standard search engines rely solely on exact keyword matches, a customer searching for "heavy duty washdown casters for food processing" is often met with empty results pages. This friction directly causes [**Inventory Debt**](/blog/inventory-debt-large-catalog-shopify-revenue), where high-margin specialty inventory sits undiscovered in deep catalog sub-collections.
