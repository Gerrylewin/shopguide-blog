## The Specification Wall in Technical Supply Catalogs

Managing a high-SKU catalog in the HVAC, plumbing, and mechanical fittings vertical presents an immense customer discovery challenge. When contractors, facility managers, or trade technicians search for replacement valves, heat exchangers, or ductless mini-split condensers, matching exact technical specifications is critical.

A single digit discrepancy in pipe thread pitch (NPT vs. BSPP), system voltage (208V vs. 230V), or tonnage rating transforms a standard order into an expensive return cycle. Traditional faceted search grids rely on static tags that collapse under complex query parameters. When a customer inputs "3-ton 16 SEER2 heat pump package unit with 3/4-inch suction line", standard keyword engines routinely drop the customer onto a page with dozens of mismatched SKUs or return zero results.

This mismatch forces shoppers to abandon carts or inundate customer support desks with manual fitment verification calls.
