Look.

The stack is not the problem. The silence between the apps is.

Klaviyo owns email. Judge.me or Rivyo owns reviews. Smile.io owns points. Slack owns the team. Alia owns the popup offer. None of them talk to the shopper at the same second.

So the visitor closes the popup. Never sees the review that would have closed the sale. Never hears they have points sitting there. And when they finally ask for a human, your team gets a blank ticket.

That is what merchants mean when they complain about app fatigue. Not "I hate apps." They hate paying for tools that never share context.

ShopGuide integration partners exist for that gap.

## One feature: partner context inside the conversation

ShopGuide is an on-page agent. You add the ShopGuide Chat section in the theme editor. The dashboard lives in the Shopify admin sidebar. Product truth comes from the [Shopify Catalog API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql) during the chat, not from a spreadsheet you uploaded last month.

The partner layer sits on that same conversation.

Keep Klaviyo. Keep your review app. Keep loyalty. ShopGuide reads those systems when the shopper is actually deciding, and writes back only where the partner supports it.

Most pairings go live when both apps are installed. Slack is the exception. That one needs an incoming webhook URL in ShopGuide settings.

You are not buying a fifteenth discovery widget. You are giving the existing stack a mouth.

The current list lives on the [ShopGuide integrations page](https://integrations.yourshopguide.com/shopify).

## How ShopGuide itself is wired in

Three connections. Not thirty.

**1. Theme block.**  
ShopGuide Chat is a theme section. Appearance, launch messages, and page visibility live in the app admin. One storefront surface. Not a new script for every partner.

**2. Shopify Admin.**  
Home, Dashboard, Test your Guide, Chats, Path Explorer, Appearance, Integrations, Settings. Chat-attributed revenue shows up there. You do not leave Shopify to see if the agent sold anything.

**3. Live Catalog API.**  
Stock, variants, and price come from Shopify during the conversation. That is why a sold-out SKU should not get recommended as available. Details in the [live Catalog API post](/blog/live-catalog-api-keeps-agent-answers-accurate-high-sku-shopify).

Partners plug into that same thread. They do not get their own chat window.

The Integrations screen in the admin is where review-app tokens live. Slack is a webhook. Path Explorer is the Klaviyo sync button for known emails. That is the wiring. Catalog for truth. Theme block for the shopper. Partners for context your catalog does not store.

## Why merchants care

High-SKU stores already run the standard kit: email, reviews, loyalty, a support channel. Public threads keep repeating the same three complaints.

Scripts stack up. Dashboards do not share data. Two apps in the same category fight each other.

On r/shopify_growth, operators say the stack got bigger than the team. On r/shopifyDev, the 2026 version of the same gripe is messy integrations and tools that do half-jobs. The useful test is not "how many apps." It is whether the shopper ever sees the thing you paid for at the moment they hesitate.

ShopGuide does not ask you to rip Klaviyo out. It asks the agent to use Klaviyo. Same for reviews and points.

The value is boring and useful:

- The review that answers "does this run large" shows up in chat instead of on a tab the shopper never opens.
- Points become a sentence instead of a forgotten widget.
- A closed popup is not a dead offer if the agent can bring the incentive back.
- A human handoff includes the transcript, cart, and customer fields instead of "please describe your issue."

On a 200-SKU boutique that is polite. On a 10,000-SKU catalog it is how the same question stops becoming a ticket.

## Honest limits

Say this out loud before you flip any switches.

ShopGuide does not replace Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, or Slack. If the partner app is missing or misconfigured, there is nothing to read.

Reviews in chat are only as good as the reviews you have collected. Empty review catalog means empty social proof.

Smile.io only helps once the shopper is identified. A first-time anonymous visitor has no points to surface.

Slack is one-way. ShopGuide posts context into a channel. Your team still resolves in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin. You do not run the whole conversation from Slack replies.

Klaviyo sync from Path Explorer needs a reachable email. It will not invent a list from anonymous sessions.

The agent cannot invent metafields that live only inside another app and never in Shopify. Dirty product data still produces thin answers.

That is the deal. Context in the conversation. Not a new operating system for your store.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are ShopGuide integration partners?**
They are the apps ShopGuide can read from or write to during a guided session: Klaviyo, Judge.me, Rivyo, Smile.io, Alia, and Slack, plus the native Shopify Catalog API. The public list is on the [integrations page](https://integrations.yourshopguide.com/shopify).

**How is ShopGuide integrated into Shopify?**
Three places. A theme block on the storefront. A native app in the Shopify admin for analytics and settings. Direct reads from the Shopify Catalog API during chat. Partner apps attach to that same conversation.

**Do I have to replace my current apps?**
No. Keep the apps that already work. ShopGuide reads them. It does not ask you to rip Klaviyo or Judge.me out.

**How do I turn a partner on?**
Install both apps. Most pairings activate when both are live. Slack needs a webhook URL pasted into ShopGuide settings. Review apps use a token on the Integrations screen.

**Will this add more scripts and slow the storefront?**
ShopGuide is one theme block. It does not install a second copy of Klaviyo or Judge.me. Partner apps keep their own scripts. The integration is the agent reading those systems, not another pixel farm.

**What if I only use some of the partners?**
Use the ones you have. A store on Judge.me and Klaviyo does not need Rivyo or Alia for the agent to be useful.

**Does Slack let my team reply inside Slack?**
No. Slack gets a structured alert with context and a link to the transcript. Resolution stays in Shopify or the ShopGuide admin so the history lives in one place.
