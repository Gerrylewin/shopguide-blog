You already spend money to get people on the site.

Ads. SEO. Email. Influencers. The traffic lands. Then a big chunk of it stares at a grid of 3,000 products, tries a search that returns nothing useful, and leaves.

That is the discovery gap on high-SKU stores. You know it exists. You just cannot prove what happens when you close it.

ShopGuide fixes the measurement problem with one feature: revenue attribution.

## What revenue attribution actually does

Every time the on-page agent helps a shopper, the session gets a unique ID. When that shopper places an order, the system links the conversation to the order in Shopify.

You see:

- Assisted revenue (the dollars that came from guided sessions)
- AOV on assisted orders versus the store average
- Which products sold because the agent surfaced them
- How many new customers came through the agent

It is not a vanity metric. It is the same order data you already trust, just tagged with the conversation that helped close it.

No extra tracking pixels. No guessing. The agent lives inside your theme as a Shopify app block and talks to the live Catalog API. The attribution rides on the same order pipeline.

## Why this matters for high-SKU catalogs

On a store with a few hundred products you can sometimes eyeball the impact of a new search app. On 5,000 or 15,000 SKUs the noise is too high. Filters disappear above 5,000 products in a collection. Search is literal. Long-tail items stay buried. You cannot tell if the chat tool is moving the needle or just chatting.

Revenue attribution cuts through that. Merchants running ShopGuide see assisted orders that are consistently larger. Case studies show baskets with 25% more items, AOV lifts in the 20-40% range on guided sessions, and a meaningful share of total revenue coming from the agent on active stores.

The point is not that every visitor talks to the agent. The point is that the ones who do buy more, and you can see the exact dollars.

## The honest limits

Attribution only works if the customer finishes the order in a way that keeps the session linked. If they open a new browser, clear cookies mid-journey, or complete checkout days later on a different device, the link can break. That is normal for any session-based system.

It also only measures what the agent touched. It does not claim credit for every order on the store. That is intentional. You get clean numbers on guided discovery, not inflated "AI revenue" claims.

If your product data is thin, the agent has less to work with and the attribution numbers will reflect that. Better data still drives better results.

## Where this fits in agentic commerce

John Collison has said keyword search is a ridiculous way to find things to buy. He is right. The shift is from matching strings to understanding intent and acting on it.

Harley Finkelstein put the merchant angle simply: retail is entering its agentic era. AI agent-based shopping can level the playing field for brands that are not Amazon.

Revenue attribution is the part that lets you measure the level. You keep running the same traffic channels. You add an on-page agent that actually knows the catalog. Then you look at the dashboard and see whether the guided sessions are paying for themselves.

That is the job. Not more chat volume. More clear revenue from the visitors you already have.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does ShopGuide attribute revenue to a conversation?**  
Each assisted session receives a unique identifier. When the shopper completes an order, that identifier is associated with the Shopify order so the dashboard can show assisted revenue, AOV, and product mix.

**Does this replace my existing analytics?**  
No. It sits alongside Shopify analytics and any other tools you use. It adds the conversation-to-order link that most chat tools leave out.

**Will I see higher AOV on every assisted order?**  
Not every single one. The pattern across merchants is that guided sessions produce larger baskets because the agent removes uncertainty and surfaces complementary items that match the customer intent. The dashboard shows the real delta for your store.

**Is the data real-time?**  
Orders appear in the ShopGuide dashboard as they flow through Shopify. There is no separate nightly sync for the core attribution numbers.

**Does this work on free plan?**  
The free plan includes the core agent and basic analytics. Full revenue attribution detail and unlimited scale sit on the paid plans. Check the current Shopify App Store listing for exact limits.

**What if a customer chats then buys later?**  
Attribution is strongest when the order happens in the same session or with the tracking intact. Cross-device or multi-day journeys can still convert, but the direct link may not always attach. That is standard for session-based measurement.
