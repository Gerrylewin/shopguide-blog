## The Technical Specs Wall in Commercial Refrigeration

Commercial refrigeration and HVAC parts present an exceptionally tough e-commerce challenge. A single catalog contains thousands of overlapping variables:

- **Refrigerant Compatibility:** R-404A, R-134a, R-407C, R-448A, and R-290.
- **Electrical Specs:** Voltage (115V, 208-230V, 460V), Phase (Single vs. 3-Phase), and Hertz (50Hz vs. 60Hz).
- **Physical Dimensions & Fittings:** Sweat connections, ODF, rotolock valves, and capillary tube sizing.
- **Application Temperature Ranges:** Low Temp (freezers), Medium Temp (cooler displays), and High Temp (AC units).

When contractors search, they rarely format their query to match the exact string inside your Shopify product title. A contractor looking for a thermostatic expansion valve (TXV) might search by valve body series, port size, or tonnage. If your store catalog requires exact phrase matching, the buyer hits a brick wall.

This is where traditional filters fail. A standard filter dropdown menu with 30 options across 5 categories overwhelms mobile technicians standing on a hot roof.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does traditional Shopify search fail on commercial HVAC and refrigeration parts?**
Traditional search relies on character-matching strings. When a technician searches for _"3 HP R404A compressor 208V"_, standard search fails if your product title orders terms differently (e.g., _"Copeland Compressor, 208/230V, 3HP, R-404A"_). **Agentic shopping** uses semantic reasoning over catalog metafields to understand intent regardless of word order or syntax.

**How does agentic shopping handle OEM part cross-referencing?**
By connecting directly to the **Shopify Catalog API**, ShopGuide indexes OEM cross-reference tables stored in your product descriptions, tags, and custom metafields. When a customer enters an old or competitor part number, the agent maps it to the compatible active SKU in your store.

**Can the AI agent suggest complementary installation parts like capacitors and filter driers?**
Yes. Agentic shopping acts like an experienced parts counter rep. When a contractor selects a commercial compressor, the agent automatically reviews technical dependencies and asks if they also need a compatible liquid line filter drier, sight glass, or potential relay, boosting overall AOV.

**Does installing an on-page AI agent slow down my Shopify Plus storefront?**
No. ShopGuide is integrated natively via Shopify Theme App Blocks. It executes catalog queries asynchronously through high-performance API endpoints, maintaining rapid page load speeds even for stores with over 50,000 SKUs.

**What happens if a technician asks a question about a custom-engineered refrigeration system?**
If a query exceeds standard catalog parameters, ShopGuide can trigger a structured handoff via **Slack** or your support desk. The agent passes the full conversation transcript, cart context, and technician requirements directly to your sales engineers for rapid follow-up.
