## Why Standard Search Fails Technical Adhesive Catalogs

Traditional keyword search indexes match character strings rather than structural chemistry. When B2B engineers shop for industrial sealants, cyanoacrylates, or two-part epoxies, their buying criteria depend on multi-variable matrices:

1. **Substrate Material Compatibility:** An epoxy engineered for ABS plastic will fail on polypropylene or anodized aluminum without surface primers.
2. **Viscosity & Gap Fill Limitations:** Thixotropic gels hold vertical gaps up to 0.5mm, whereas low-viscosity liquid penetrants require tight slip fits under 0.05mm.
3. **Cure Dynamics & Environment:** Anaerobic compounds cure strictly in the absence of air and presence of active metal ions, requiring chemical activators for passive metal substrates like zinc or stainless steel.

When these engineering variables are stored across custom metafields, technical datasheets (TDS), and variant lists, legacy collection filters collapse.
